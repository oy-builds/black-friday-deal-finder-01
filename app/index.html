How these deals were checked

Each price was read on 27 November 2026 from the named store or from a deals report that names the store (9to5Toys, 9to5Mac, Product Bible, PopSci, DealNews, AppleInsider, Digital Trends, 2ndShift, Home Automation Weekly and the stores’ own Black Friday announcements). The “usual” price is the store’s list price or the typical selling price the source quoted; where a list price looks inflated, the verdict says so. Prices change and stock runs out — the store link always shows the live price, so check it before you buy.

Verdict key: NEW LOW lowest price the sources have tracked for this item. STRONG PRICE a genuine cut, well below the usual price. ROUTINE SALE the discount is real but the “was” price is inflated or the price was this low before.
